SB – Savings Account

Interest rates are subject to change as per Government of India / Department of Posts notifications.

RD – Recurring Deposit

RD interest is compounded quarterly; each monthly instalment earns interest only for the months it stays deposited. It is not simple interest.

TD – Time Deposit

TD interest is payable annually. The annual TD interest can be credited to the linked Savings Account. If that amount remains in SB, it may earn SB interest according to the applicable rules.
